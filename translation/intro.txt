Long, long ago,
young Goku sought
the Dragon Balls.
Gather all seven,
it was said, and
Shenron would
grant one wish.

After many
adventures he had
all seven, but to
foil a villain, a
silly wish was
made instead.

Shenron sleeps a
year after each
wish. Goku spent
it training with
Master Roshi and
took second place
at the Tenkaichi
Budokai. At the
next one he beat
Piccolo and won.

Then the Saiyans
attacked Earth.
Piccolo and many
others fell.
Goku's side drove
them off, but
with Piccolo dead
Earth's Dragon
Balls were gone,
and with them any
hope of reviving
the fallen.
But Krillin had
overheard the
Saiyans. Piccolo
came from Namek,
and Namek had its
own Dragon Balls!
King Kai agreed.
Mr. Popo found
Kami's old ship,
able to reach
Namek in a month.
So Bulma, Krillin
and Gohan set off
to seek Namek's
Dragon Balls.
Meanwhile Vegeta,
gravely wounded,
went to Frieza's
Planet No. 79 to
heal. There, Cui
let slip that
Frieza was on
Namek, after the
Dragon Balls.
Vegeta raced
to Namek...
