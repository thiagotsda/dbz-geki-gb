# opening narration (resource 18): one line per screen line, max 17 columns, pages of 8 lines
Long, long ago...
Gather all seven
Dragon Balls, and
Shenron appears
to grant a wish.

Son Goku set out
to find them.
After many
adventures he
gathered them,
but they were
nearly misused,
so he made a
dull wish.

Shenron rests a
year after each
wish. Meanwhile,
Goku trained with
Master Roshi and
took second place
at the Tenkaichi
Budokai.
At the next one
he beat Piccolo
and won it all.

Then the Saiyans
attacked Earth.
Piccolo and many
others died.
Goku's friends
drove them off,
but with Piccolo
dead, Earth's
Dragon Balls were
gone, and so was
any way to bring
the fallen back.
But Krillin had
heard the Saiyans
say Piccolo came
from Namek, and
that Namek had
Dragon Balls too!
King Kai agreed.
Mr. Popo found
Kami's old ship,
which can reach
Namek in a month.
So Bulma, Krillin
and Gohan set off
for Namek to seek
the Dragon Balls.
Meanwhile Vegeta,
badly wounded,
went to Frieza's
Planet No. 79 to
heal. There, Cui
told him Frieza
was on Namek,
after the Balls.
Vegeta rushed
to Namek...
